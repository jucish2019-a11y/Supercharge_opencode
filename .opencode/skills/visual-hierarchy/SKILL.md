## Quick reference

The hierarchy tools in order of power:

1. **White space** — Creates separation and emphasis. Tight within groups, generous between.
2. **Weight** — Bold vs regular creates hierarchy within text. Maximum 2 weights per design.
3. **Size** — Large vs small creates visual levels. Use a type scale (4-5 sizes per page).
4. **Color** — Accent vs neutral creates focal points. Use sparingly; when everything is accent-colored, nothing is.

The 3-second test: If you can't identify the most important element in 3 seconds, the hierarchy is wrong.
